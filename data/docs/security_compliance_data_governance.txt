Security, compliance, and data governance at Trevor Tan Incorporated (TTI) are foundational pillars of the company’s infrastructure and product philosophy, especially given its enterprise customer base across highly regulated industries such as finance, healthcare, logistics, and government adjacent sectors, and TTI maintains compliance with major industry standards including SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, which require continuous auditing of internal controls, access management systems, incident response procedures, and data handling practices across all systems, and security is implemented using a layered defense in depth model where multiple independent security controls operate across network, application, infrastructure, and data layers, and all data in transit is encrypted using TLS based protocols while all data at rest is encrypted using AES 256 encryption standards across storage systems, databases, and backups, and access control is enforced through strict role based access control systems ensuring least privilege principles where employees and services only have access to the minimum data required for their function, and privileged access to sensitive systems is protected through multi factor authentication, time bound access grants, and centralized identity management systems with full audit logging, and TTI operates a centralized secrets management system for storing and rotating API keys, credentials, and cryptographic materials, and direct embedding of secrets into code or configuration files is strictly prohibited and actively detected through automated scanning in CI/CD pipelines, and data governance policies define strict classification levels including public, internal, confidential, and restricted data categories with corresponding handling rules for storage, transmission, retention, and access control, and customer data is generally treated as confidential or restricted depending on contractual agreements and regulatory obligations, and in machine learning workflows TTI enforces data lineage tracking across all datasets used for training, validation, and inference ensuring full traceability of data sources, transformations, and usage history which supports reproducibility and auditability of machine learning models, and the LabelFlow system includes built in audit trails and approval workflows to ensure dataset quality control and compliance with labeling guidelines, and incident response is managed by a dedicated security operations function with 24/7 on call coverage for critical systems and clearly defined severity classification levels that determine escalation paths, response times, and mitigation strategies, and in the event of a security incident the process includes immediate containment, forensic investigation, root cause analysis, remediation, and customer notification when required by contractual or regulatory obligations, and TTI also ensures compliance with regional privacy regulations including GDPR in Europe and PDPA in Singapore, supporting capabilities such as data subject access requests, right to deletion requests, and consent management frameworks for personal data processing, and data residency requirements are supported through region specific deployments allowing enterprise customers to ensure that sensitive data remains within required geographic boundaries, and security is integrated throughout the software development lifecycle with static application security testing, dynamic vulnerability scanning, dependency risk analysis, and infrastructure configuration validation embedded directly into CI/CD pipelines, and regular internal and external security audits are conducted to ensure ongoing compliance with regulatory frameworks and industry best practices, and overall TTI’s security and governance approach is designed to enable safe deployment of machine learning systems in highly sensitive enterprise environments while maintaining transparency, accountability, and strong protection of customer and organizational data assets across global operations.
