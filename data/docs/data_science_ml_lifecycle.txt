The data science and machine learning lifecycle at Trevor Tan Incorporated is a structured end to end process designed to ensure that machine learning models are not only accurate in experimentation but also reliable, scalable, and maintainable in production environments, and the lifecycle begins with problem definition where business stakeholders, product managers, and machine learning engineers collaborate to clearly define the objective, success metrics, constraints, and expected impact of a machine learning solution, and this stage emphasizes translating ambiguous business problems into measurable machine learning tasks such as classification, regression, ranking, clustering, or generative modeling depending on the use case, and once the problem is defined the next stage is data acquisition where relevant datasets are identified, collected, and ingested from internal systems, third party sources, or real time data streams, and TTI places strong emphasis on data governance at this stage ensuring that all data usage complies with internal policies and external regulations including privacy and security requirements, and after data acquisition the data preparation stage begins where raw data is cleaned, normalized, transformed, and structured into formats suitable for machine learning workflows, and this includes handling missing values, outlier detection, feature engineering, encoding categorical variables, and constructing training datasets with proper labeling when supervised learning is involved, and data versioning is enforced using internal systems to ensure reproducibility and traceability of experiments, and the next stage is model development where data scientists and machine learning engineers experiment with different algorithms, architectures, and hyperparameter configurations using the Cortex Train platform which provides distributed compute resources and experiment tracking capabilities, and during this phase models are evaluated using offline metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score, ROC AUC, mean squared error, or other domain specific metrics depending on the problem type, and model training is often iterative with multiple experiments conducted in parallel to identify optimal configurations, and once a candidate model is identified it moves to the validation stage where more rigorous testing is performed including cross validation, robustness testing, bias and fairness evaluation, stress testing under edge cases, and simulation of production like conditions to ensure model stability, and after validation the model enters the deployment stage using Cortex Deploy where it is packaged into a containerized service and deployed into staging environments before gradual rollout into production through canary deployments or A B testing strategies, and once in production the model enters the monitoring stage where Cortex Monitor and Pulse systems continuously track model performance, input data drift, latency, system health, and business KPIs to ensure ongoing reliability, and if performance degradation is detected automated alerts are triggered and incident response procedures may initiate rollback or retraining workflows depending on severity, and retraining is a critical part of the lifecycle where models are periodically updated using new data to maintain accuracy and adapt to changing real world conditions, and this retraining process can be automated or manual depending on the model criticality and business requirements, and throughout the entire lifecycle governance and compliance are enforced through audit logs, dataset lineage tracking, model versioning, and approval workflows ensuring that every model deployed in production is fully traceable and explainable, and collaboration is a key aspect of the lifecycle where data scientists, engineers, product managers, and operations teams work together through documentation driven processes including RFCs, experiment reports, and post deployment reviews, and overall the machine learning lifecycle at TTI is designed to bridge the gap between experimental data science and industrial grade production systems ensuring that machine learning models deliver consistent, measurable, and safe value in real world enterprise environments.
