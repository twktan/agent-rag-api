Trevor Tan Incorporated employee onboarding and training program is a structured multi phase experience designed to integrate new hires into the company’s technical, cultural, and operational environment while ensuring they are able to contribute effectively to production systems and collaborative workflows as quickly and safely as possible, and the onboarding process begins before an employee’s official start date with a preboarding phase where new hires receive access to initial documentation through the Atlas knowledge platform, including company overview materials, engineering philosophy guides, security training prerequisites, and role specific reading lists tailored to their department whether engineering, product, research, operations, or business functions, and during this preboarding stage employees are also provisioned with secure accounts, hardware shipping if required, access credentials, and identity management setup through the company’s centralized authentication system, and on the first day of employment employees participate in a structured orientation program that introduces them to Trevor Tan Incorporated’s mission, organizational structure, product ecosystem including the Cortex Suite, and core operational principles such as documentation driven development, asynchronous communication, and ownership based engineering culture, and this orientation is typically conducted in a hybrid format with both live sessions and recorded modules to accommodate global time zones given the company’s distributed workforce, and following orientation employees enter a role specific onboarding track which differs depending on whether they are joining engineering, machine learning, data science, product management, or business operations teams, and engineering onboarding includes deep training on the company’s development stack including Python, Go, and Rust usage patterns, Kubernetes based infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines through Forge, observability systems via Pulse, and internal tooling such as Atlas, Prism, and Nimbus sandbox environments, while non engineering roles receive training tailored to their workflows including product lifecycle management, customer engagement systems, enterprise sales processes, or operational governance depending on their function, and a key component of onboarding is the completion of mandatory security and compliance training which covers data classification policies, secure coding practices, incident reporting procedures, privacy regulations including GDPR and PDPA, and internal security protocols such as secrets management and access control systems, and employees are required to complete interactive assessments to ensure understanding before gaining full production system access, and after initial training employees are assigned an onboarding buddy and a mentor, where the buddy is typically a peer in a similar role responsible for helping navigate day to day questions and organizational norms while the mentor is a more senior team member focused on long term career development and technical guidance, and the onboarding process also includes participation in a simulated project environment where new hires work on a small scoped but realistic task within their team’s domain such as building a microservice, improving a data pipeline, or designing a small machine learning experiment depending on their role, and this simulation is conducted within isolated Nimbus environments to ensure safety and allow experimentation without production risk, and new hires are encouraged to submit design documents even for small projects to reinforce the company’s RFC driven engineering culture early in their tenure, and throughout the first ninety days employees undergo structured check ins at regular intervals including two week, one month, and three month reviews to assess integration progress, technical ramp up, cultural alignment, and any blockers to productivity, and feedback from these sessions is used to adjust onboarding materials and provide additional support where needed, and training at TTI is not limited to initial onboarding but continues throughout an employee’s lifecycle through internal workshops, technical talks, research presentations, and mandatory annual refresher courses on security and compliance topics, and the company also provides a learning and development stipend which employees can use for external courses, certifications, or conferences to further enhance their skills, and overall the onboarding and training system at Trevor Tan Incorporated is designed to ensure that employees are not only technically prepared to contribute to complex machine learning systems but are also deeply aligned with the company’s operational philosophy, collaborative practices, and long term vision of building reliable and scalable AI infrastructure for enterprise customers.
