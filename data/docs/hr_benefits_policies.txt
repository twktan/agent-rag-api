Human Resources at Trevor Tan Incorporated (TTI) is designed to support a globally distributed, remote first workforce while ensuring consistency in employee experience, compliance with regional labor laws, and alignment with the company’s culture of ownership, transparency, and high performance, and TTI employs individuals across more than twenty five countries with primary operational hubs in Singapore, San Francisco, and London, and the HR framework is structured to provide both standardized global benefits and localized adjustments depending on jurisdictional requirements, and all full time employees receive a core benefits package that includes comprehensive healthcare coverage encompassing medical, dental, and vision insurance where applicable, along with life insurance and disability coverage based on regional eligibility, and retirement benefits are provided through country specific pension or retirement savings plans with company contributions designed to remain competitive within each local market, and TTI promotes strong work life balance through flexible time off policies including a minimum of twenty paid vacation days per year excluding public holidays, along with flexible sick leave policies that prioritize employee health and trust without excessive administrative burden for short term absences, and parental leave policies are inclusive and globally standardized at a high baseline level with extended leave provisions for primary caregivers and additional flexibility for secondary caregivers including maternity, paternity, and adoption leave options, and the company places significant emphasis on learning and professional development through an annual learning stipend provided to each employee which can be used for certifications, online courses, academic programs, conferences, and technical training relevant to their role, particularly in fields such as machine learning, distributed systems, cloud computing, software engineering, and leadership development, and internal training programs are regularly conducted by senior engineers, researchers, and product leaders to ensure continuous knowledge sharing across teams, and performance management at TTI is structured around quarterly check ins and biannual performance reviews focusing on qualitative feedback, impact assessment, and goal alignment rather than purely numerical scoring systems, where employees define objectives aligned with team and company goals and progress is tracked through regular one on one meetings with managers emphasizing growth, contribution, and scope expansion, and compensation and promotion decisions are based on a combination of performance, demonstrated impact, technical complexity of contributions, and organizational needs rather than tenure alone, and employee wellness is a core HR priority with mental health support services, counseling access, wellness stipends for fitness or personal health activities, and flexible working arrangements designed to accommodate global time zones and individual circumstances, while actively discouraging burnout through workload monitoring and sustainable engineering practices, and internal culture at TTI is built on asynchronous communication, documentation driven collaboration, and respect for deep work time, with most communication occurring through written systems rather than excessive meetings, and recruitment processes are designed to evaluate both technical expertise and cultural alignment through multi stage interviews including technical problem solving, system design assessments, and behavioral interviews focused on collaboration, ownership, and adaptability, and diversity and inclusion are core HR principles with active efforts to ensure fair hiring practices, equitable opportunity distribution, and representation across teams, and overall HR at TTI aims to create a high trust, high performance environment where employees are supported in their professional growth while maintaining flexibility, autonomy, and a healthy work life balance across a globally distributed organization.
